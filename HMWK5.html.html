Riemann vs Lebesgue integration, and the normal CDF

Es 1 – Riemann and Lebesgue sums

Riemann (midpoint): split the domain into n subintervals, sum f(midpoint)·width. Lebesgue-style: split the range into k levels, estimate the measure of each preimage {x : f(x) in level} with N = 200 000 domain points, sum level·measure.

Log–log plot of |error| against n (Riemann, blue) or k (Lebesgue, red; N = 200 000 fixed). Errors below 1e-16 are drawn at 1e-16.

Es 2 – Normal CDF Φ(x)

Methods: trapezoid and Simpson on Φ(x) = ½ + ∫₀ˣ φ; erf relation Φ(x) = ½[1 + erf(x/√2)] with the Abramowitz–Stegun 7.1.26 approximation; power series Φ(x) = ½ + φ(x)(x + x³/3 + x⁵/(3·5) + …); series + continued fraction for the tails. Reference: Python math.erfc (double precision), matching R pnorm (e.g. Φ(1.96) = 0.9750021).
